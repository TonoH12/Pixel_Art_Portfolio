This artwork was made for Lospec Dallies, a daily pixel art challenge where you create pixel art based on a color palette and a word given. For this artwork I got the color palette Pink & Green, and the keyword "Boredom." This one took me some time to decide what to draw, since boredom is an abstract concept. I finally decided on drawing a pose I sometimes make when I'm bored.

Color Palette: Pink & Green
