This artwork was made for Lospec Dallies, a daily pixel art challenge where you create pixel art based on a color palette and a word given. For this artwork I got the color palette Christmas 23, and the keyword "Exotic." When I was looking for exotic things to draw, I came across an image of a Grey Crowned Crane, which I chose to draw.
