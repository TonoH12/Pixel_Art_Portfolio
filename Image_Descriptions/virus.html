This artwork was made for Lospec Dallies, a daily pixel art challenge where you create pixel art based on a color palette and a word given. For this artwork I got the color palette Ghost Town, and the keyword "Virus." I first created an virus looking thing that was supposed to be located within an organism, but the background ended up being outer space themed since it looked cooler that way.

Color Palette: Ghost Town
